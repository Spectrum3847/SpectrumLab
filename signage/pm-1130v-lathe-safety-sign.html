Machine Shop Safety

PM‑1130V Lathe

“The Blue Lathe”

It Spins. It Grabs.

Gloves, sleeves, hair, and jewelry can be pulled in — and don't come back out.

Required
Goggles always on
Hair tied back
Closed-toe shoes
Never
Gloves
Loose items

The Non‑Negotiables

Chuck Key Out — Always

Left in, it's a projectile.

Never Leave It Running

Spindle off before you step away.

Chips: Brush, Stopped

Never fingers. Never while it's turning.

Spindle Speed

Aluminum≤ 2000 RPM
Steel≤ 1200 RPM

Ceilings for small stock. Bigger diameter → slower; parting and knurling at ⅓ turning speed. Always check the tach — never guess.

E‑Stop First

Know it. Hit it first.

Training Required

Signed off & mentor in the lab.
