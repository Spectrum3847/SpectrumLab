Beyond the [StackTech towers](/storage/stacktech-drawers/), the shop's tools live in a fleet of rolling toolboxes — each with a job, each with labeled drawers. This page is the directory: which box holds what, with a clickable drawer map for the big ones.

| Box | What it holds | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red Toolbox (US General Pro) | The main hand-tool set — printed drawers **1–16** | Machine shop |
| Orange Toolbox (US General Pro) | Metrology, machinist tooling, stock, gloves & PPE — printed drawers **1–13** | Under the Evolution chop saw |
| Blue Toolbox (US General) | Fab support — calipers, CNC router & xTool MetalFab accessories | Between the WAZER and the Gweike |
| Lathe T-Stak stack | Lathe support drawers + the rivetnut kit | Under the lathe bench |
| T-Stak pit kits | Pre-packed competition tool duplicates | Storage room, by the door |
| Craftsman cabinet | Under-bench specialty storage | Under the butcher-block bench |
| Storage cart | Chain hoist, belt sander, vac attachments | Machine shop |

<SafetyWarning type="tip" title="Two red boxes, two different jobs">
The shop has **two** red US General Pro boxes and people mix them up constantly. The tall two-stack chest with drawers 1–16 is the **hand-tool** box. The wide box under the chop saw — the one with orange tape around its edges — is the **Orange Toolbox**, and it holds the measuring instruments, machinist tooling and the glove/PPE stock. If you're looking for calipers, micrometers, a drill vise or a box of nitrile gloves, you want the orange one.
</SafetyWarning>

## Red Toolbox — Hand Tools

The red US General Pro two-stack chest is the primary hand-tool bank. Every drawer carries a printed number and name, so "it's in drawer 7" is a complete answer. There are **sixteen drawers, numbered 1–16**, and the numbers run bottom-up: 1–8 are the lower cabinet, 9–13 the upper chest, and 14, 15 and 16 are the three narrow drawers side by side across the top.

## T-Stak Competition Pit Kits

Stacked by the storage-room door are the T-Stak **pit kits**: pre-packed duplicates of the most-used tools. They exist for one reason — so the shop set never leaves the building. Two rules:

- **Pit kits go to events. Shop tools do not.** If the pit kit is missing something, fix the pit kit; don't raid the red toolbox on load-out night.
- After an event, restock the pit kit *before* it goes back in the stack, and return any strays to their shop drawer.

## House Rules for Every Box

- **Tools return to their labeled drawer** — the same meeting you took them out.
- Drawers closed when you walk away. An open drawer at shin height is a shop injury on a timer.
- Don't migrate tools between boxes to "improve" the system solo — propose the change, then relabel properly per the [labeling standard](/storage/label-makers/).
- A drawer with a number and no name (red 3) or no label at all (most of the blue box) is a **job**, not a mystery. Print the label.
- Lost, broken, or dull? Say so. Replacements get tracked on the [Consumables Ledger](/inventory/consumables/).
