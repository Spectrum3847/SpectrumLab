## Red Toolbox — Hand Tools

The red US General Pro two-stack chest is the primary hand-tool bank. Every drawer carries a printed number and name, so "it's in drawer 7" is a complete answer. The drawer map below matches the printed numbers 1–16, followed by the unnumbered drawers (gloves, drill vise, calipers, micrometers).

Inside, nearly every drawer uses 3D-printed sawtooth racks and labeled bins so a missing tool is obvious at a glance:

<LabGalleryImage src="combination-wrenches-drawer.jpg" alt="Drawer of combination and ratcheting wrenches in 3D-printed sawtooth organizers" caption="Drawer 11 — combination wrenches in sawtooth organizers, handles color-coded by size with tape." />

An empty slot in a sawtooth rack means a tool is out. Before you leave, empty slots should mean *nothing*.

## Lathe T-Stak Drawers

Under the lathe bench, a DeWalt T-Stak stack labeled **Lathe Drawers** keeps the lathe's supporting cast together: drill bits and spare hardware, measuring tools and grease, electrical and tape, and the rivetnut kit.

<LabGalleryImage src="lathe-drawers-tstak.jpg" alt="DeWalt T-Stak stack labeled Lathe Drawers under the lathe bench" caption="The lathe T-Stak stack — support consumables and hardware within reach of the machine." />

Turning tools, workholding, and lathe metrology live in the lathe toolbox itself — mapped on the [Lathes page](/forming/lathes/).

## T-Stak Competition Pit Kits

Stacked by the storage-room door are the T-Stak **pit kits**: pre-packed duplicates of the most-used tools. They exist for one reason — so the shop set never leaves the building. Two rules:

- **Pit kits go to events. Shop tools do not.** If the pit kit is missing something, fix the pit kit; don't raid the red toolbox on load-out night.
- After an event, restock the pit kit *before* it goes back in the stack, and return any strays to their shop drawer.

## Under-Bench Craftsman Cabinet

The black Craftsman drawer cabinet under the butcher-block bench holds specialty items that don't earn a spot in the main chests (its most famous drawer label: *Air Wedges*). The Klutch sheet-metal shear and the drill-press vises live on the bench above it.

## House Rules for Every Box

- **Tools return to their labeled drawer** — the same meeting you took them out.
- Drawers closed when you walk away. An open drawer at shin height is a shop injury on a timer.
- Don't migrate tools between boxes to "improve" the system solo — propose the change, then relabel properly per the [labeling standard](/storage/label-makers/).
- Lost, broken, or dull? Say so. Replacements get tracked on the [Consumables Ledger](/inventory/consumables/).
