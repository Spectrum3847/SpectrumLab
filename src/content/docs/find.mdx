## If it isn't here

Two possibilities, and they need different fixes:

- **It exists but isn't catalogued.** Several drawers are still uncatalogued — the [Blue Toolbox](/storage/toolboxes/#blue-toolbox--fabrication-support) is mostly unlabelled, and drawers 4, 6, and 8 of the [Orange Toolbox](/storage/toolboxes/#orange-toolbox--metrology-machinist-tooling--ppe) have never been logged. Tell a mentor what you found and where, and it gets added.
- **We don't have it.** Check the [Tool Inventory](/inventory/tool-list/) to be sure, then say so in the team Slack rather than assuming somebody already ordered one.

## Putting things back

Search only works if things go back where the search says they are. The rules that keep this page honest:

1. **One home per tool.** If it has a labelled drawer, that drawer is the only correct place for it.
2. **Put it back at the end of the session, not the end of the build.** A tool that lives on a bench for three weeks is a tool nobody can find.
3. **A new home needs a label and an entry.** If something genuinely needs a new drawer, [label it](/storage/label-makers/) and get it into `src/data/storage-locations.json` so it shows up here.

## How this page works

Everything comes from two data files in the site repo — `src/data/storage-locations.json` and `src/data/consumables.json` — rendered at build time and filtered in your browser. There is no server, so it's fast and it works on shop wifi at its worst.

You can link straight to a search with `?q=`, e.g. [`/find/?q=reamer`](/find/?q=reamer). That's what makes drawer QR codes possible.
