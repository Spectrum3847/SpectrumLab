Welcome to the lab. This is where Spectrum 3847 designs, builds, breaks, and rebuilds robots — and this page is your map. Watch the tour video, then read the zone-by-zone breakdown below to learn what lives in each area and where its documentation is.

The lab is in **Agee Hall at Strake Jesuit**: the classroom / computer lab is **room 3105** and the machine shop is **room 3103**, with the kitchen / AV room, the robot closet and the storage closet off the same suite. Those are the five areas Spectrum's own [Intro to the Spectrum Lab](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1205YhdRqVWo_SNL98QDpuGrRmZiaUV5GLA4yd4YoWfw/preview) orientation deck walks new members through — this page is the long version of it.

<SafetyWarning type="warning" title="Before you touch anything">
New in the lab? Read [PPE & The Glove Policy](/safety/gloves-and-ppe/) and [Emergency Protocols](/safety/emergency/) first. Every machine in the shop requires training before you run it — the SOP pages tell you what sign-off each one needs.
</SafetyWarning>

The video was filmed for the 2025 season, so a few machines have arrived since — the written tour below reflects the shop as it is today.

## Classroom

The first room you walk into. Big team meetings, design sessions on the big screen, whiteboard arguments about intake geometry, and general hangout time all happen here. It's also where the daily build blog gets written — Spectrum publishes its engineering notebook publicly all season.

When a design review ends with "go make it," everything else on this page is where that happens.

## Additive Farm

The 3D-printing corner runs nearly nonstop during build season, feeding printed parts to both the main team and the junior team. The fleet today: two Bambu Lab X1-Carbons on 0.6 mm nozzles (Bambu 1 with a 4-slot AMS plus an AMS-HT, Bambu 2 with an AMS-HT), a Bambu Lab H2D on 0.4 mm with an AMS Pro and an AMS-HT, a Bambu X2D on 0.4 mm (with more on order), and twelve Prusa Minis in wooden cubbies under the bench.

## Machine Shop

The heart of the lab, where designs become reality. In one room: the WAZER waterjet, the xTool MetalFab fiber laser, the Gweike and Thunder CO2 lasers, the Langmuir Titan press brake, the PM-1130V lathe, the mill, and the saw wall.

<LabGalleryImage src="machine-shop-from-tool-bench.jpg" alt="Machine shop overview with lathe, bandsaw, arbor press, belt sander, and battery charging bench" caption="The machine shop from the tool bench — lathe, bandsaw, arbor press, and the battery charging area." />

<LabGalleryImage src="wazer-and-blue-toolbox.jpg" alt="WAZER waterjet next to the blue toolbox workstation" caption="The WAZER waterjet and the blue toolbox workstation — the fab corner of the shop." />

Every machine has its own SOP page — read it before you run it:

- **Waterjet:** [WAZER Pro](/waterjet/wazer-pro/)
- **Lasers:** [xTool MetalFab](/lasers/xtool-metalfab/), [Gweike Cloud M3 Ultra](/lasers/gweike-m3-ultra/), [Thunder Laser Nova 35](/lasers/thunder-nova-35/)
- **Forming:** [Langmuir Titan 25T Press Brake](/forming/langmuir-titan-25t/), [Lathes](/forming/lathes/), [Arbor Press & Broaching](/forming/arbor-press/)
- **Saws:** [Evolution Chop Saws](/cutting/evolution-saws/), [Bandsaws](/cutting/bandsaws/), [Drill Press & Bench Shear](/cutting/drill-press-shear/)

Tool storage lives here too — the [StackTech towers](/storage/stacktech-drawers/) and the [toolbox fleet](/storage/toolboxes/).

## Assembly Tables

The design and assembly tables are where subsystems come together during the season. They get gloriously messy during build — clear your spot when you leave. Photon, the team's little purple dinosaur mascot, is usually tucked in somewhere nearby, and the material cart keeps polycarbonate sheet, extrusion, and clamps within arm's reach.

<LabGalleryImage src="material-cart-polycarb-clamps.jpg" alt="Rolling material cart with polycarbonate slots, extrusion cubbies, and clamps" caption="The rolling material cart by the assembly area — labeled polycarb slots, extrusion cubbies, clamps on top." />

Working at the tables usually means [M12 cordless tools](/portable-power/m12-ecosystem/) and the [hand tool](/hand-tools/layout-measuring/) drawers.

## Powder Coat Room

A small room where parts get beautiful in shades of purple and white. The spray booth, gun, respirators, and powder shelf live here — the box-fan filter's purple dust tells you which color we shoot most.

<LabGalleryImage src="powder-booth-spray-area.jpg" alt="Powder coating booth with spray gun, respirators, powder bottles, and dusted filter" caption="The powder coating booth — gun, respirators, powder stock, and the well-used filter." />

Powder coating runs the booth fan at all times and a respirator is recommended whenever powder is airborne. It has its own SOPs: [Powder Coating](/finishing/powder-coating/), [Curing Oven](/finishing/curing-oven/), and [Surface Prep & Cleaning](/finishing/surface-prep/).

## Merch Storage

The same room doubles as merch storage — excess team t-shirts, TRI shirts, banquet supplies, and apparel boxes, plus the flammables cabinet.

<LabGalleryImage src="storage-room-apparel-flammables.jpg" alt="Storage room with apparel boxes, green flammables cabinet, and TRI shirts shelf" caption="Merch and supply storage — apparel boxes, TRI shirts, and the flammables cabinet." />

Shirts start life in the lab too — see [Graphics & Apparel](/graphics/heat-press/) for the heat press and vinyl workflow.

## Practice Room

The big open room. Each season the practice field gets constructed here for code testing and driver practice, with computers, cameras, and drive-team electronics stationed behind the plexiglass barrier. It's also the home of TRI — the Texas Robot Invitational, Spectrum's offseason event.
