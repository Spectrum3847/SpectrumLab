The Bambu fleet is the fast lane of the printer farm: two X1-Carbons — **Bambu 1** and
**Bambu 2**, each with a 4-slot AMS — and an **H2D** with an AMS HT. These are the lab's most
capable printers and the **only** machines allowed to run PA-CF and PC. If a part is going on
the competition robot, it probably comes off one of these.

## Safety

<SafetyWarning type="warning" title="Hot and fast">
The nozzle runs up to 300–350 °C and the toolhead moves at up to 1000 mm/s. Never reach into a
printer while it is printing, homing, or calibrating. Pause the print and wait for motion to
stop before touching anything inside.
</SafetyWarning>

- The bed runs up to 110 °C on engineering materials. Let plates cool before grabbing them
  bare-handed.
- Keep lids and doors set for the material (see each printer's card on the
  [Filament Guide](/3d-printing/filament-guide/)): lid off / door open for PLA and TPU,
  closed for PA-CF and PC.
- IPA is flammable — never spray it on a hot bed. Wipe plates on the bench, not in the machine.
- Do not defeat or ignore a paused print: if the machine paused itself, it found a problem.
  Read the screen before resuming.

## Software & Workflow — Bambu Studio

1. Open **Bambu Studio** on the shop PC and make sure you are on the team login.
2. **Pick the right device.** In the Device tab, select Bambu 1, Bambu 2, or the H2D. Check its
   camera view first — make sure the plate is empty and nobody else's job is queued.
3. Import your model, orient and arrange it (see
   [Print Settings & Supports](/3d-printing/print-settings/)).
4. **Set the plate type in the slicer to match the plate physically in the printer.** Textured
   PEI, Cool Plate, and Engineering Plate have different first-layer settings — a mismatch
   ruins the first layer or damages the plate.
5. **Map filaments to AMS slots.** Click the filament chips and sync with the AMS. Bambu spools
   auto-identify via RFID; third-party spools must be assigned manually — set material type
   *and* color so the next person can trust the display.
6. Slice, sanity-check the preview (time, filament use, support placement), then **Send** to
   the selected printer. Enable timelapse if you want it; leave AI failure detection on.
7. Monitor the first layer from the camera view or the Bambu Handy app. Do not walk away until
   layer one is down clean.

No dedicated consumables ledger for the printers — everything reorders through the links below:

- **Filament** — see the [Filament Guide](/3d-printing/filament-guide/) for what to buy and the
  reorder links on every material card.
- **90%+ IPA and paper towels** — plate cleaning. Lives at the printer bench.
- **Glue stick** — release/adhesion layer for PC and PA-CF plates.
- **Spare hotends/nozzles and wiper pads** — Bambu store; keep at least one spare hardened
  hotend per X1C on the shelf.
- **AMS desiccant packs** — swap when the AMS humidity indicator climbs.

## Operating Instructions

### Loading the AMS

1. Cut the filament end at ~45° so it feeds cleanly.
2. Open the AMS lid, feed the end into an empty slot until the feeder grabs it and the slot
   LED confirms.
3. Seat the spool so it unwinds toward the feed tube without rubbing the AMS walls.
4. Bambu spools identify themselves via RFID. For third-party spools, set material and color in
   Bambu Studio or on the touchscreen immediately — an unlabeled slot is a trap for the next
   person.

### Unloading

1. From the touchscreen or Studio, select the slot and choose **Unload**.
2. Wait for the printer to purge and retract fully, then pull the spool and re-bag or dry-box
   it. Tape the filament end to the spool — loose ends cause tangles that kill overnight prints.

### Starting and watching a print

1. Send the job from Bambu Studio (workflow above).
2. The printer auto-levels and, on the X1Cs, **lidar-inspects the first layer**. If it flags a
   problem, stop — do not override. Fix the plate (clean, reseat, correct type) and restart.
3. The **AI camera watches for spaghetti failures** and pauses the print if detached plastic is
   flying around. If you get the notification, inspect via camera before resuming or canceling.
4. When the print finishes, let the plate cool a couple of minutes (parts self-release as the
   plate cools), then flex the plate to pop parts off. Never chisel parts off a plate on the
   bed — you will bend the plate or gouge the PEI.

### Nozzle / hotend swap (mentor sign-off required)

The X1-Carbons swap the **whole hotend assembly**, not just a nozzle tip:

1. Power off and let the hotend cool completely.
2. Pop off the magnetic toolhead front cover and disconnect the hotend fan connector.
3. Loosen the hotend retaining screws, swap in the replacement hotend, reconnect, and refit the
   cover. Follow the Bambu Wiki guide the first time — with a mentor.
4. Confirm nozzle size and type on the touchscreen and in Bambu Studio.
5. **Run full calibration (lidar + vibration) after every nozzle change** before the machine
   returns to service.

The H2D uses guided quick-swap nozzles — follow the on-screen maintenance flow. The same rule
applies on every machine: **carbon-filled filament only ever runs through hardened nozzles.**
The stock X1C hotend is hardened steel; if a brass or unknown hotend has been fitted, it does
not print PA-CF.

### Common failures

- **First-layer inspection fails** — dirty or wrong plate 90% of the time. Wipe with IPA,
  verify plate type in the slicer, restart.
- **Spaghetti detection triggers** — part came unstuck mid-print. Clear all debris from the
  bed and around the toolhead before restarting; check the plate prep and consider a brim.
- **AMS retry / feed error** — tangled spool or filament ground down by the feeder. Unload,
  trim the damaged section, check the spool for cross-winds, reload.
- **Clicking extruder / underextrusion on CF materials** — wet filament or worn nozzle. Dry
  the spool first; if it persists, the nozzle is due for replacement (weekly wear check is on
  the maintenance schedule).

## Cleanup & Maintenance

After your print:

- Remove parts **and** purge waste; leave the plate empty and clipped in.
- Return spools you unloaded to their dry box or bag.
- Wipe the plate with 90%+ IPA every few prints, or immediately after glue-stick use.

On the schedule (see [Maintenance Schedules](/maintenance/schedules/)):

- **Every few prints:** wipe plates with 90%+ IPA.
- **Weekly:** check nozzle wear on the CF printers (part of the farm walk-down).
- **Monthly:** clean or replace the nozzle wiper pads.
- **After any nozzle change:** full lidar + vibration calibration before the next job.
