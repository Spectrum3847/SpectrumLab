Sequence

  1. Face the end. A saw cut is not flat and a drill will walk on it.
  2. Spot drill first — always. A twist drill cannot start its own hole.
  3. Pilot if the final hole is over 1/4 in.
  4. Peck 1 diameter deep, retract fully, clear the chip, repeat.
  5. Counterbore or tap, then deburr the mouth.

Depth

+0.3×D A 118° drill point adds about 0.3 × the drill diameter of extra depth past the full-diameter shoulder. Zero the quill scale by touching the faced end, then add your depth.
No DRO The 2-axis DRO reads the cross slide and carriage — not the quill. Drilling depth comes off the tailstock graduations.
Stop Blue chips, squealing, or smoke = too fast or dry. Spindle off, oil it, drop the RPM. Clear chips with the hook, never fingers.

Facing to a dimension — use Z

  1. Face one end clean. That end is now the reference.
  2. Flip, chuck on the faced end, seat it square.
  3. Face the second end just clean.
  4. Measure overall length with calipers, spindle stopped.
  5. Zero Z on that freshly faced end. Z now counts length removed — one-to-one, nothing to halve.
  6. Face in to Z = −(what you owe), in two bites. Re-measure between them.
  7. Deburr both ends or nothing will sit flat.
X mode Z is length, always one-to-one. X can read radius or diameter — a factor of two. Leave it in diameter mode.

A run of identical parts

Setup Cut the batch to one rough length at the chop saw stop block first. Then hold one Z zero for the whole run — check the first, the last, and one in the middle.

RPM — low range, interrupted cut

ShaftAluminumSteel
1/2 in hex450–650250–350
3/8 in hex600–800300–450
Tool 1.0 mm insert for 1/2 in rings · 0.8 mm for 3/8 in. One tool per size, each pre-set in its own block. Never the 1.5 / 2 mm parting inserts — they leave the ring 0.024–0.034 in of slop. And never groove-tool a part-off.

Sequence

  1. Face the end and get the shaft to final length first.
  2. Chuck short. Minimum stick-out; the 3-jaw grips hex properly.
  3. Zero Z at the shaft end, then touch off the corner and zero X (diameter mode).
  4. Move to position on Z, then LOCK THE CARRIAGE. The DRO puts you there; the lock keeps you there.
  5. Plunge to X = 0.468 (0.348 on 3/8). Slow, steady, no dwelling.
  6. Caliper the groove, spindle stopped. Trust the caliper over the readout.
  7. Test-fit a real ring. It should snap in, sit flat, not rock.
  8. Deburr the six corners or the ring will not seat.
Center Above center it rubs; below center it digs and snaps the insert. Six corners a revolution leaves no margin — set height with the Pro Lathe Gage.
